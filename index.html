Our story

A little white café with a lot of heart.

Terry & Perry started with a simple idea: our community deserved a café where the coffee is taken seriously and the room feels like a deep breath. Clean lines, soft daylight, textured stone counters, and a menu built for every part of your day.

Our baristas pull every shot to order. Our matcha comes straight from Uji, Kyoto. And whether you're here for a quick Americano on your way through or a slow afternoon with friends over croffles, there's a seat waiting for you.
